Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information EZDL ("we", "us", "our") collects when you use the EZDL app or the ezdl.net website, and how we use it. The short version: EZDL runs locally, we collect the minimum needed to run your account and keep the app working, and we never see the videos or audio you download.

1. How EZDL handles the media you download

All downloading and converting happens directly on your own computer. When you paste a link, the App talks to the source site and saves the file straight to your chosen folder — it is never uploaded to, or routed through, our servers. Your download history is kept only in a local file on your own computer — we never see it, and it's never sent to us.

2. Information we collect

WhatWhenWhy
Email address & password (hashed) When you create an account To sign you in and identify your subscription
Subscription status & Stripe customer/subscription IDs When you subscribe to a paid plan To know which features your account can use
Anonymous error reports — the error message (with any URL stripped out), app version, and OS When a download or tool fails To find and fix bugs; not linked to your account

Payment card details are entered directly with our payment processor, Stripe, and never pass through our servers or the App.

3. Information that stays on your device

Some settings never leave your computer: your output folder, proxy / VPN configuration and credentials, browser cookie selection (used to download content you're logged into), and the download queue itself. These are stored in a local settings file and are not transmitted to us.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't run ads, ad trackers, or third-party analytics SDKs in the App or on the Site.
  • We don't sell your data to anyone.
  • We don't read the contents of files you download or convert.

5. Where your data is stored

Account data is stored with our database provider, Supabase. Payment data is held by Stripe. Both are bound by their own security and privacy practices as our data processors.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account for as long as it exists. To delete your account and associated data, email us at support@ezdl.net — we'll confirm once it's done. Anonymous error reports aren't linked to an account and can't be deleted individually.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us at support@ezdl.net to exercise any of these.

8. Children's privacy

EZDL isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at support@ezdl.net.
