Support

Need a hand?

Most answers are below. Can't find yours? Email us directly and we'll sort it out.

Email support@ezdl.net

We typically reply within a day or two.

Do I need an account to use EZDL?

No. There's no sign-up, no email/password, no account of any kind. Install the app and it opens straight in. Your free trial and, later, your license key are tied to your device, not to an account.

How does the free trial work?

You get 24 hours of full access from the first time you use the app. That start time is anchored on our server against your device, so uninstalling and reinstalling doesn't reset the clock.

How do I get a license key?

Buy a plan on the Pricing page. Your key shows up on the confirmation page right after payment, and we also email it to you.

How do I activate a key in the app?

Open the sidebar menu at the bottom left and choose License. Paste your key into the box and click Activate. It takes effect immediately, no restart needed.

How many devices can one key be used on?

Up to 3 at a time. If you need to move a key to a new device once you're at the limit, open the License page on one of the existing devices and click "Deactivate this device" to free up a slot.

Does the Yearly plan auto-renew?

No. Nothing about EZDL auto-charges you, ever. When a Yearly key is about to expire, you renew manually by purchasing again. There's no subscription to remember to cancel.

Is my data private?

Yes. EZDL runs entirely on your own machine: video and audio are fetched straight from the source site to your disk and processed locally. We never see, host, or touch the files you create.

What sites does EZDL work with?

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Twitch, Vimeo, Reddit, SoundCloud, and over 1,750 others. If a site you need isn't working, let us know (see the troubleshooting question below).

Is EZDL available for Mac or Linux?

Windows only for now. macOS and Linux builds are planned but not scheduled yet.

Why does Windows warn me about the installer?

Code-signing for the installer is in progress. Until it's live, Windows SmartScreen and some antivirus tools may flag a new, unsigned installer even though nothing's wrong. Click "More info" → "Run anyway" in the SmartScreen prompt to continue.

What do I do if a download or conversion isn't working?

Source sites change how they work without notice, which can break a specific download or feature. Email us the URL you tried and any error message shown, and we'll take a look.

Can I get a refund?

Refund requests are handled case-by-case: email support@ezdl.net within 14 days of your purchase.
